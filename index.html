Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect on my first medspa visit in Lubbock?

You can expect a friendly talk, a close look at your skin, and a clear plan. The team will ask about your goals, your skin history, and what you use at home. Then they will suggest one or two steps that fit you best. Most first visits are calm and easy, and you will leave knowing what comes next.

How do I pick between Botox and fillers?

Botox is often used for lines that move, like frown lines, while fillers add volume where skin has lost fullness. Many people use both, in different spots, for a balanced look. The team will look at your face, listen to your goals, and help you choose the mix that looks most natural for you.

Will a medspa treatment hurt?

Most treatments cause only mild discomfort. Some feel like a small pinch, some feel warm, and some feel like a light snap. The team uses numbing cream or cooling when it helps, and they will check in with you during the visit. If you are nervous, tell them. They can slow down and talk you through each step.

How long does it take to see a change?

It depends on the treatment. Fillers often show a change the same day. Botox builds over a few days. Lasers and microneedling build over weeks as collagen grows. The team will give you a fair timeline for your plan, so you know when to look for change and when to come back.

Can I go back to work after a treatment?

Most people can go back to work the same day. Some treatments leave redness or mild swelling for a day or two. The team will tell you what to expect for your exact treatment, and they will give you simple aftercare steps. If you have a big event, book a little early so your skin has time to settle.

Is Korean beauty care right for my skin type?

Korean beauty care focuses on hydration, gentle steps, and healthy skin, which suits many skin types. The team will look at your skin and adjust the plan to fit you. If a treatment is not right for you, they will say so and suggest a better option. Your skin, your tone, and your goals all shape the plan.
